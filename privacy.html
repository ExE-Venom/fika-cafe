Privacy Policy

Transparency and privacy protection at FIKA COFFEE CO. Learn how we handle information submitted on our static site.

1. Overview & General Commitment

FIKA COFFEE CO. ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website fikacoffee.co as a static web application. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy of our visitors while providing a seamless, beautiful digital experience.

Because our site runs strictly as a client-side static web application without a custom backend server or database, we do not store, sell, or profile your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information Submitted via Enquiry Form

When you send us an enquiry or reservation message on our Enquiry Page, we collect the details you voluntarily provide:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Subject / Enquiry Purpose
  • Message Content & Timestamp

Before any enquiry form submission is transmitted, our system enforces an explicit, mandatory consent checkbox requirement:

"☐ I consent to having the data filled in this form shared with the cafe owner so they can respond to my enquiry."

This consent record is transmitted alongside your message to ensure a legal record of permission.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

Standard browser log information (such as IP address, browser type, device type, and referring URL) may be processed transiently by static hosting infrastructure (such as GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel) for DDoS protection and security monitoring.

3. Cookie & LocalStorage Usage

We distinguish strictly between Essential Site Operations and Optional Performance Cookies:

Category Purpose Storage Type
Essential Cookies Stores your cookie consent choice so the banner does not re-appear on every page reload. Browser localStorage (fika_cookie_consent)
Analytics Cookies Aggregates anonymous page view traffic metrics to optimize site performance and menu load times. Optional (Loaded ONLY if Accepted)

5. Third-Party Services & Form Handlers

To maintain a lightweight static web application without dedicated servers, we utilize trusted third-party providers:

  • Formspree (Static Form Routing): Form submissions are processed by Formspree to route your email directly to cafe management. Formspree acts as a data processor adhering to GDPR standards.
  • Google Maps Iframe: Embedded location map on our home page. Subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
