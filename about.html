Born in Hauz Khas

The Art of Taking a Break

FIKA COFFEE CO. was created out of a desire to build a sanctuary amidst Delhi’s fast-paced energy. In Swedish, Fika is not just drinking coffee—it is a mandatory daily ritual of pausing your work, catching your breath, and connecting deeply with the people around you.

When we discovered this quiet corner in Hauz Khas Village facing the lush foliage of Deer Park, we knew it was the perfect canvas. We designed FIKA with warm oak timber, soft Scandinavian lighting, and wide open windows that let in natural breeze and morning birdsong.

Whether you come in solo with your favorite notebook, meet a long-time friend over hot cinnamon buns, or spend an afternoon working beside our plants, FIKA is your home away from home.

Conscious Coffee

Ethical Sourcing & Artisanal Craft

Every cup tells a story of direct-trade partnerships with shade-grown coffee estates in Southern India.

🌱

100% Indian Single Origins

We source directly from high-elevation estates in Araku Valley (Andhra Pradesh) and Chikmagalur (Karnataka). Beans are roasted in small batches to preserve delicate notes of stone fruit, cacao, and wild spices.

🤝

Fair Trade & Local Bakes

Our coffee farmers earn above fair-trade market rates. Every pastry—from our signature cardamom buns to sourdough croissants—is hand-braided daily using local organic flour and cultured butter.

♻️

Eco-Conscious Promise

All take-away cups, straws, and food containers are 100% plant-based and compostable. We proudly offer Swedish Oat Milk alternatives at zero extra charge.
